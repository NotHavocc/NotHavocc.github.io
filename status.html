HAM Corner

some useful HAM radio links and embeds (best viewed on large screens)

> some other useful links

- https://www.sigidwiki.com/ (examples and info about lots of signals, everything from VLF to UHF)

- https://www.dxinfocentre.com/ (useful station lists, tropospheric and frequency maps etc.)

- https://www.n2yo.com/ (satellite tracking and finding site)

- https://aprs.fi/ (map of APRS stations and known senders))

- https://www.eibispace.de/ (useful website about SW))

- https://udxf.nl/ (shortwave news and info) )
